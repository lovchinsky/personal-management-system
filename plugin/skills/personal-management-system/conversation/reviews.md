# Reviews

`knowledge/reviews.md` says what each review covers and how to judge it. When the person asks only what needs attention, start from the daily control.

- Show what you found and ask about it. When the person says yes to a change, make it with Notion's own tools in their name. A decision on a stored proposal goes through `conversation/changes.md`.
- Open proposals: list them with `approval_read` and read each one before you show it.
- An Inbox item the person wants to clarify now: `conversation/capture.md`.
- A change of the `Mode` of an Area of Focus: when you ask what "normal" means for it now, say that you will remember the answer as its acceptable state, and record what they answer (`knowledge/memory.md`).
