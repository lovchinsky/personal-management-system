# deep_review: does the shape of the system still fit the person

Look at Identities, Principles and the set of Areas of Focus, and show the person what is worth taking up when they sit down for the deep review. The packet is your only effect. Budget: Reads: 22 in all (Notion 16, GBrain 6).

## Work

1. `runtime_read` gives today's date.
2. Look at what the questions of the deep review need (`knowledge/reviews.md`).
3. Read the timelines on the anchor pages of the Identities and Areas of Focus your matters are about, as the budget allows (`knowledge/memory.md`); when episodes in their timelines share something, follow `knowledge/lessons.md`.
4. Queue the packet (`reviewing.md`) even when you found nothing: the person asked for this review, and its time has come. It carries the few matters that most deserve the person's time now, each with what you saw, or else the deep review's questions (`knowledge/reviews.md`) about the Identities and Areas of Focus you read; a single failure is not a matter about an Identity. They are the agenda for a conversation with you, not questions to answer in a reply, and `ask` says so. A change to an Identity or a Principle is the person's to make.

## Finish

Return the result (`result.md`, `reviewing.md`); its `detections` stay empty. After a packet it looks like this (`<…>` stands for a value from the trigger):

```json
{
  "outcome": "review_sent",
  "operating_phase": ["Reflect"],
  "scope": { "object_type": "Review", "object_id": "<trigger event_id>" },
  "detections": [],
  "user_facing_or_notification_message": "<the packet's noticed, why_it_matters and ask, joined>"
}
```
